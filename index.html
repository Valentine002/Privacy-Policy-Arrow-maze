Privacy Policy

♟️ Arrow Maze Updated: October 20, 2026
Welcome to Arrow Maze, a game developed by Venticy. We believe that solving intricate logic puzzles should be a purely offline, uninterrupted experience. This policy explains how your privacy is handled while you navigate the dense mazes of the game.

1. Zero Data Collection

Arrow Maze is designed with a "privacy-first" architecture. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or telemetry.

The game does not require you to:

  • Create an account, log in, or authenticate
  • Provide an email address, name, or phone number
  • Grant access to your location, contacts, or device files
  • Submit payment information
✓ 100% Offline & Private Everything stays on your device. Your level progress, unlocked modes, and star counts are saved locally on your device's internal storage. We have no servers, no databases, and no way to view your gameplay.

2. Gameplay & Data

Arrow Maze features two distinct logic modes, both of which operate entirely locally:

🌀 Classic Mode
The original dense maze. Pure logic. Your progression, level unlocks, and completion status are saved locally.
🔐 Twist Mode
Navigate locks, keys, and heavy arrows. Your performance and unlock conditions (e.g., earning stars in Classic) are processed offline.

No analytics, tracking pixels, or background data collection are running while you play.

3. Third-Party Services

Arrow Maze does not intentionally integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or crash reporting services that track personal data.

If future updates introduce third-party services (such as an ad network to support development), this Privacy Policy will be updated in advance to explain exactly what data is accessed and how it is handled.

4. App Permissions

To function correctly, Arrow Maze may request minimal, non-sensitive permissions:

  • Storage / Media: Only used locally if the game needs to save your progress or cache level assets to your device.

We do not access your photos, microphone, camera, or contacts.

5. Children's Privacy

Because Arrow Maze does not collect any personal information, it is safe for users of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly (or unknowingly) collect data from anyone.

6. Changes to This Policy

If Arrow Maze receives future features that alter its data practices, this Privacy Policy will be updated. Any changes will be reflected directly on this page with an updated date at the top.
